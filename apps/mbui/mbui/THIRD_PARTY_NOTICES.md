## MoonBit core's math (FreeBSD msun, musl)

- What: `src/dmath/` (sin, cos, tan, the inverse functions, exp, ln, pow and
  their helpers): MoonBit core's own MoonBit ports (its `math` package's
  non-JS files, moonbit 0.1.20261016), taken whole so that they run on every
  target, js too, in place of the engine's `Math.*`.
- From: https://github.com/moonbitlang/core (`math/`), itself after FreeBSD's
  msun (Sun Microsystems' fdlibm) and musl.

## Jetpack Compose and the Android Open Source Project

- What: `src/ui/scroll.mbt` (and the expected values in
  `src/ui/scroll_wbtest.mbt`, `scripts/scrollplot.py`): Compose's
  `VelocityTracker1D` (Lsq2, `polyFitLeastSquares`), `FlingCalculator` and
  `AndroidFlingSpline` (the `OverScroller` spline), `android.widget.EdgeEffect`'s
  stretch, HWUI's `StretchEffect` and Compose's

  `src/motion/spec.mbt` after Compose's `SpringSimulation`,
  `SpringEstimation.kt` and its easing curves. `scripts/ComposeRef.java` and
  `scripts/compose_ref.sh` run Compose's own classes from Gradle's cache (not
