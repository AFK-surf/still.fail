The three front-on device drawings are adapted from [PommePlate](https://github.com/ephread/PommePlate): iPhone
X/XS/11 Pro Space Gray, iPad Pro 11 / iPad Air 4 Space Gray, and MacBook Pro 16-inch Space Gray. PommePlate dedicates
its mockups (SVG, PNG and Sketch files) to the public domain under CC0 1.0; its logo has different terms and is not used.

The SVGs here add a mask that makes the original screen outline transparent. The screen rectangle and its corners
come from the original artwork; its other paths have not been redrawn. Put the screen picture underneath the hardware,
not over the notch or bezel. The screen and window interface must come from the picture.
