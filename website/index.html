01 号池01 Account pool

账号放进一个池子，
额度一点不浪费Every account in one pool, no limit left unused

每台 station 有一个号池。新对话按「本周额度要花多快才不浪费」分给各个号：快重置、剩得多的多分，但不会全压在一个号上。Each station keeps a pool of accounts. Most new chats go to the ones that would otherwise waste their weekly limit (soonest reset, most left), but no account takes them all.

自动挑号Picks the account

跳过登录失效、密钥被拒或额度用尽的号；5 小时额度用到 80% 的排最后。Skips accounts that are signed out, have a refused key or are out of quota. One past 80% of its 5-hour limit goes last.

换号不丢活Moves without losing work

进行中的对话尽量留在原账号。必须换时，agent 自己读回聊天记录；后台命令还在跑时不提前换。A running chat stays on its account where it can. When it has to move, the agent reads back its own history, and it never moves early while background commands still run.

station 之间共享Shared between stations

一台 station 登录的账号可以分享给同一 workspace 的其他 station，别的电脑不用再登录。A station can share its signed-in accounts with the rest of its workspace, so the other computers don't sign in again.

停下的活会告诉 agentStopped work is reported

换号时若停了后台任务，下一轮开头告诉 agent 停了哪些；Claude 的 subagent 能从停下的地方接着跑。If a switch stops background work, the agent is told what stopped at the start of its next turn, and Claude's subagents carry on from where they were.

80%5 小时额度用到这里，新对话先给别的号of a 5-hour limit, and new chats go to other accounts first
5 分钟5 min有人在看或有对话在跑时，每 5 分钟读一次额度between quota reads while someone is watching or a chat is running
75%上下文到压缩点的 75% 才提前换号，少丢上下文of the way to compaction before a chat moves early, so little is lost
64每个 workspace 最多 64 台 station，账号可以互相分享stations per workspace at most, each able to share its accounts

安全Security

对话留在你的电脑上Your chats stay on your own computers

手机和电脑直接连到你的 station，连不通时走加密中继。云端只管账号和中继，读不到内容。Your phones and computers connect straight to your stations, or through an encrypted relay when they can’t. The cloud runs accounts and relays, and can’t read your content.

  • 点对点连接Peer to peer

    先直连，连不通再走中继。不用开端口、配 VPN 或内网穿透。Direct first, a relay only when that fails. No ports to open, no VPN, no tunnels.

  • 数据在你的电脑上Data on your machines

    对话、文件和记录存在 station 本地，不进云端数据库。Chats, files and history live on the station, not in a cloud database.

  • 安全模式Security mode

    开启后，成员、设备和 station 须经可信设备签名才被接受，单凭云端加不进任何人。Once on, members, devices and stations need a trusted device’s signature. The cloud alone can’t add anyone.

  • 密钥库Vault

    安全模式下，agent 用的凭据加密保存，经人批准才交出。In security mode, the keys agents use stay encrypted until a person approves.

  • 开源Open sourceMIT
  • 官方 CLIOfficial CLIs不改 agent，不绕过它们的限制Agents run unmodified, within their own limits

常见问题FAQ

问得最多的Asked most

号池怎么决定用哪个账号？How does the pool choose an account?

只在启用了所选模型、还能用的账号里挑；5 小时额度用到 80% 的排最后。其余按本周额度要花多快、5 小时额度还剩多少来分：快重置、剩得多的号多分。Only accounts that have the chosen model and still work are considered; those at 80% of their 5-hour limit go last. The rest are weighted by how fast each must spend its weekly limit and what's left of its 5-hour limit, so accounts that reset soon with more left get more chats.

中途换账号，会丢上下文吗？Does switching accounts lose context?

换号后 agent 会读回之前的聊天记录。对话尽量留在原账号，不能用了才换；提前换只挑上下文刚压缩过或快要压缩的时候，后台命令在跑时不换。After a switch, the agent reads its earlier history back. A chat stays on its account until that account can't be used; it moves early only right after or near a compaction, and never while background commands are running.

和直接在终端里用 Claude Code 有什么不同？How is it different from Claude Code in a terminal?

终端里的 agent 只能坐在那台电脑前用。monoid 把每台电脑上的 agent 放到一处：账号进号池，用手机或 Slack 交代和查看，每台电脑的负载一处看全，密钥经你批准才给 agent 用。agent 本身不变。A terminal agent is used at that computer. monoid brings the agents on all your computers into one place: accounts share a pool, you direct them from your phone or Slack, see every computer's load, and approve each key before an agent uses it. The agents themselves are unchanged.

要付费吗？Does it cost anything?

目前免费。套餐限制 workspace 和成员数，每个 workspace 最多 64 台 station。agent 用你自己的订阅账号或 API 密钥。Free for now. Plans limit workspaces and members; a workspace holds up to 64 stations. Agents use your own subscriptions or API keys.

代码和对话会传到云端吗？Do my code and chats go to the cloud?

不会。对话、文件和记录留在你的电脑上；云端只管账号和中继，中继转发的是它看不到内容的加密数据。No. Chats, files and history stay on your computers. The cloud handles accounts and relays, and a relay passes on encrypted data it can't read.

电脑要一直开着吗？Does my computer have to stay on?

要。agent 在你的电脑上运行，电脑得开着、联网。station 随登录启动，不用开着窗口。Yes. Agents run on it, so it has to be on and online. The station starts at login; no window has to stay open.

不在同一个网络里，能连上吗？Does it work across networks?

能。设备之间直连，连不通就走加密中继，不用配端口、VPN 或内网穿透。Yes. Devices connect directly, or through an encrypted relay when they can't. No ports, VPN or tunnels to set up.

会影响我的 agent 账号吗？Does it affect my agent accounts?

monoid 运行的是各家 agent 的官方命令行，和你在终端里用一样：不改它们，也不绕开它们的限制。monoid runs each agent's official CLI, just as you would in a terminal: unmodified, and within its own limits.

iPhone 和 iPhone Duo 能用吗？Can I use it on an iPhone or iPhone Duo?

能。App Store 版即将上架，现在用网页版，添加到主屏幕后像 App 一样打开。station 上的 iOS 模拟器（包括 iPhone Duo 的两块屏和折叠姿态）可以在对话旁远程操作。Yes. The App Store app is coming; until then, add the web app to your home screen and it opens like an app. The iOS Simulator on a station, iPhone Duo's two screens and fold included, can be driven beside the chat.

开源吗？Is it open source?

开源，MIT 许可，代码在 GitHub。Yes, under the MIT license. The code is on GitHub.
